# Jordan Rivera

## Situation
Jordan Rivera is a 34-year-old single parent of two children (household of 3) in
Oakland, CA. In March they had an emergency appendectomy at **Bay Area General**,
a nonprofit hospital.

## The bill
- Total outstanding: **$4,200**
- Roughly 60 days past due; a collections notice was mailed last week.
- No itemized bill has been requested or reviewed yet.

## Finances
- Gross annual household income: **~$31,000** (roughly 150% of the federal
  poverty level for a household of 3).
- **Uninsured** — lost employer coverage after a February layoff.
- Other debts: ~$3,000 in credit-card balances; currently behind on rent.

## Hardship
- Laid off from a warehouse job in February; doing part-time gig work since.
- Sole provider for two children (ages 6 and 9).

## Goals (in priority order)
1. Qualify for **charity care / financial assistance** — income is near the line.
2. Failing that, an **income-based discount** plus an **interest-free payment plan**.
3. **Pause collections** while an assistance application is reviewed.

## Notes for the advocate
- Bay Area General is a **nonprofit**, so **IRS 501(r)** applies: it must maintain
  a written Financial Assistance Policy (FAP) and cannot bill FAP-eligible
  patients more than the amounts generally billed to insured patients.
- Request an **itemized bill** to check for errors before agreeing to any amount.
